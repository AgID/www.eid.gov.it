eIDAS regulation and eIDAS version 2.0

eIDAS regulation and eIDAS version 2.0

On 23rd July 2014, the co-legislators adopted the eIDAS Regulation, a Regulation on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market (EU Regulation No. 910-2014).

It promotes cross-border cooperation and interoperability of national electronic identification systems. Indeed, it requires European public administrations to make their services accessible across national borders using all digital identities of Member States. Furthermore, it provides a common framework for secure electronic interactions between citizens, businesses and public administrations, enhancing the security and efficacy of electronic services, e-business and electronic commerce transactions within the EU.

 

Following the eIDAS Regulation, eIDAS version 2.0 was introduced, which further enhances the digital infrastructure and the efficiency of the system. eIDAS version 2.0 provides individuals and businesses with secure and user-friendly digital identity solutions that operate seamlessly across all EU Member States, enhancing data security and establishing a level playing field for qualified trust services across the EU. The new rules for qualified trust service providers have been refined to ensure a clearer and more harmonised regulation. They also introduce electronic attestation of attributes, website authentication certificates, management of qualified devices for remote electronic signature (or electronic seal) creation, electronic archiving and electronic records. eIDAS version 2.0 also introduces the EU Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI) that allows users to store, manage and share their data, credentials and identity attributes, with online and offline access.

What is the Italian eIDAS-Node?

What is the Italian eIDAS-Node?

The Italian eIDAS-Node enables users to access online services provided by public administrations or private individuals in EU Member States using the digital identity of another Member State. By activating the eIDAS Login, administrations that make their services available online at national level also extend the use of these services to EU citizens via their home country's digital identity. At the same time, other EU member states, through their eIDAS-Nodes, allow Italian citizens with digital identities to access their services.

How does the eIDAS-Node work?

How does the eIDAS-Node work?

The functioning of the eIDAS-Node is described below through the case of an European user requesting an online service from another Member State.

  1. Initial need

    A Spanish citizen wants to register online his new address in Italy.

  2. Communications start

    The public administration communicates with the national eIDAS-Node to verify the identity of the Spanish citizen.

  3. Cross-border communication

    The Italian eIDAS-Node transmits the request to the eIDAS-Node in Spain.

  4. Identity check

    Spanish identity providers and attribute providers verify the identity of the Spanish citizen.

  5. Resolution and user access

    The Spanish citizen can access the online service after the verified identity has been sent to the Italian administration.
