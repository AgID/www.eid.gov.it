eIDAS login portal

The eIDAS-Node enables cross-border interoperability of digital identities and allows all European citizens to access Member States' online services using their national digital identity.

This platform describes the operational mechanism of cross-border authentication and provides information and guidance on the technical and administrative procedures. These procedures must be followed in order to implement the cross-border authentication by connecting to the Italian eIDAS-Node, as required by European regulations.

Why is eIDAS important?

Why is eIDAS important?

Through eIDAS Login, administrations ensure that citizens and businesses can use their digital identity to access online public services throughout the EU.

The Italian eIDAS-Node allows citizens to use the digital identity of one Member State to access the online services of the Public Administration of another Member State.

Which are the features of the eIDAS-Node?

The eIDAS-Node has three main features:

Ensuring interoperability

Ensuring interoperability

People and businesses can use their national eID Login to access public services in other EU countries. In two words: convenient and practical.

Ensuring security

Ensuring security

It reduces the risk of identity theft or misuse of personal information through high levels of system security. Privacy is guaranteed.

Providing confidence

Providing confidence

Transactions made with eIDAS Login outside national borders have legal validity and the same legal status as traditional paper-based processes, regardless of the device used. No matter where you are.

The eIDAS Regulation and eIDAS version 2.0

The eIDAS Regulation and eIDAS version 2.0

The Regulation on Electronic Identification and Trust Services for Electronic Transactions in the Internal Market, or more simply the eIDAS Regulation (electronic IDentification Authentication and Signature), helps businesses, citizens and public authorities to conduct secure electronic interactions.

The eIDAS Regulation is essential because:

 

It allows people to access services with their digital identity.

People and businesses can use national electronic identification systems to access online public services in other European Union countries.

It creates a single European market for trust services.

It ensures that these services work across national borders and have the same legal validity as their paper counterparts.

eIDAS version 2.0 builds on the foundations of the Regulation and refines some of its aspects. Indeed, it moves the EU towards an even more secure, user-centric and inclusive digital identity landscape and improves the efficiency of the internal market, cross-border and cross-sector services. It also plays a key role in achieving the objectives set out in the 2030 Digital Compass.

How can you log in with eIDAS?

How can you log in with eIDAS?

Recognising and logging in with eIDAS is easy: administrations that allow access to their online services with a digital identity display a button with the standard English wording "Login with eIDAS" in white on a blue background. The text is always followed by the logo of the European flag. These features ensure instant recognition, for a fast and secure login to digital services.
