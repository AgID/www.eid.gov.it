Enable eIDAS login

This page contains information on how to configure the acceptance of eIDAS digital identities through the interconnection with the Italian eIDAS-Node managed by AGID. The procedures to be followed are both technical and administrative, and all communication will take place through the dedicated support system and via the e-mail address indicated below. The Notice no. 01-2018 “Notes for the deployment of the EIDAS LOGIN in Public Administrations” constitutes an operational reference for the configuration of systems related to public administrations as Service Providers of the Public Digital Identity System (SPID) within elDAS.

Technical and administrative procedure

Technical and administrative procedure

The Italian eIDAS-Node operates as a virtual SPID Identity Provider (IdP), so the impact on the configuration activities of the service provider joining the SPID system is minimal. To activate the eIDAS login:

  1. Submit the communication

    Request federation in a Quality Assurance environment by sending an e-mail to servicedeskeidas@agid.gov.it with the following data:

    • Name of the Organisation
    • URL of the metadata
    • URL of the page containing “Login with eIDAS”
    • Name, surname and email of the administrative contact person
    • Name, surname and email of the technical contact person
    • List of services with access via “Login with eIDAS”.

  2. Request federation in the production environment

    Once the federation in the Quality Assurance environment has been completed, it is possible to request federation in the Production environment by sending an email to the same address, attaching a copy of the SPID membership agreement.
