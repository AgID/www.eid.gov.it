Foreword

Foreword

The Agency for Digital Italy (AgID), in implementation of Regulation (EU) 2014/910 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014, has created the website https://www.eid.gov.it/ (hereinafter ‘the Website’), with the aim of describing what the technological infrastructure known as the ‘Italian eIDAS node’ is and how it works, as well as to make available to all entities providing public services after identification through a digital identity, indications on the procedures to be followed to enable them to activate interconnection to the ‘Italian eIDAS node’.

 

The Website is dedicated to a specific theme and has purely educational and informational content.

 

This document may be modified at any time and without warning. Users are required to regularly review the latest updated version, which is available and permanently accessible from any page of the website by clicking on the "Legal notes" link.
