Node Hosting

Node Hosting

All Member States participating in the eIDAS network provide two environments. The first is the Quality Assurance environment, while the second is the Production environment. 

The following configurations will become effective as of 29 October 2026 for both the Quality Assurance environment and the Production environment. 

All Service Providers will be required to update their configurations in order to continue ensuring the availability of the eIDAS service.
